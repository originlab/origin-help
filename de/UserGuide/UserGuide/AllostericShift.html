Brief Description

Allosteric EC50 shift when X is concentration or dose.

Sample Curve

Parameter

Number: 7

Names: B, KB, alpha, Bt, Tp, EC50, s

Meanings: B=modulator concentration, KB=equilibrium dissociation constant, alpha=ternary complex constant, Bt=bottom asymptote, Tp=top asymptote, EC50=agonist concentration at half maximal, s=Hill slope

Lower Bounds: none

Upper Bounds: none

Derived Parameters
