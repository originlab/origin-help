Brief Description

Sample Curve

Parameter

Number: 7

Names: LogEC50, LogKb, Logalpha, B, A1, A2, p

Meanings: LogEC50= Logarithm of concentration of agonist at half max response without modulator., LogKb=Logarithm of equilibrium dissociation constant, Logalpha=Logarithm of ternary complex constant, B=Concentration of modulator, A1=Bottom asymptote, A2=Top asymptote, p=Hill slope

Lower Bounds: none

Upper Bounds: none
