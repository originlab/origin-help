Function









Brief Description

Evaluate a multi-curve model (Motulsky-Mahan) to predict hot ligand binding over time in the presence of varying cold competitor concentrations.

Sample Curve

Parameter

Number: 6

Names: K1, L, K2, K3, I, K4, Bmax

Meanings: K1=association rate constant of hot ligand, L=radioligand concentration of hot ligand in nM, K2=dissociation rate constant of hot competitor, K3=association rate constant of cold competitor, I=radioligand concentration of cold competitor in nM, K4=dissociation rate constant of cold competitor, Bmax=max binding capacity

Lower Bounds: K4>0, Bmax>0

Upper Bounds: none

Script Access

nlf_CompetitiveBinding(x,K1,L,K2,K3,I,K4,Bmax)
