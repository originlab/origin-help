Brief Description

Fit the EC80 or EC90, etc. instead of the EC50.

Sample Curve

Parameter

Number: 5

Names: ECF, F, Bt, Tp, s

Meanings: ECF= concentration at F percent response, F=constant percent, Bt=bottom asymptote, Tp=top asymptote, s=Hill slope

Lower Bounds: F>0

Upper Bounds: F<100

Derived Parameters

logECF=log(ECF)

span=Tp-Bt
