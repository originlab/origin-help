Brief Description

Names:LogEC50, B, pA2, SchildSlope, A1, A2, p

Meanings: LogEC50=Logarithm of EC50, B=Antagonist concentration, pA2=Negative logarithm of antagonist concentration for EC50 shift by 2, SchildSlope=Slope for competitive inhibition, A1=Bottom asymptote, A2=Top asymptote, p=Hill slope
