Introduction

Chi-square Goodness-of-Fit Test is a nonparametric statistical test used to determine whether sample data matches a hypothesized or theoretical probability distribution.

Origin supply the Chi-square Goodness-of-Fit Test app for comparing observed and expected frequencies in this test.

In the result sheet, it will output the Descriptive Statistics result, Individual Confidence Intervals table, Summary Report and Diagnostic Report with graph.
