Brief Description

Sample Curve

Parameters

Number: 5

Names: logalpha, logKb, KdHot, Allo, Bmax

Meanings: logalpha=log of cooperativity constant, logKb=log of equilibrium dissociation constant, KdHot=equilibrium dissociation constant of radioligand, Allo=allosteric modulator, Bmax=top asymptote

Lower Bounds: none

Upper Bounds: none

Derived Parameters

alpha=10^logalpha

Kb=10^logKb
